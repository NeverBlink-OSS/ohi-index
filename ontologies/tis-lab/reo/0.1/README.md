Reagent Ontology, by Institute for Formal Ontology and Medical Information Science (IFOMIS). The Reagent Ontology (ReO) adheres to OBO Foundry principles (obofoundry.org) to model the domain of biomedical research reagents, considered broadly to include materials applied “chemically” in scientific techniques to facilitate generation of data and research materials. Ontology IRI: http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/9-6-12ReO_reasoned_merge
