Ontology for Immune Epitopes, by IEDB. The Ontology of Immune Epitopes (ONTIE) is an effort to represent terms in the immunology domain in a formal ontology with the specific goal of representing experiments that identify and characterize immune epitopes. Ontology IRI: https://ontology.iedb.org/ontology/ontie.owl
