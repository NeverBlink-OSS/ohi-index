EURIO: EUropean Research Information Ontology, by Publications Office of the European Commission. The EUropean Research Information Ontology (EURIO) conceptualises, formally encodes and makes available in an open, structured and machine-readable format data about resarch projects funded by the EU's framework programmes for research and innovation.
